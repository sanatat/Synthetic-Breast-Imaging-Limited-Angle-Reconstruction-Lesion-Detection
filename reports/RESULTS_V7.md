# V7 — Volumetric limited-angle parallel-ray reconstruction (CPU)

This is a **small synthetic three-dimensional geometry diagnostic**, not a DBT cone-beam simulator and not a breast-imaging validation study.

## Setup
- Volume: 24 × 40 × 40, randomized synthetic ellipsoidal bright lesion.
- Each projection is a 2D `(detector row, detector column)` array generated by slice-wise Radon transforms around the depth axis.
- 17 projection angles, ±18° versus ±60°, Gaussian projection noise; 4 independent seeds.
- Three alternating SART steps, optionally with 3D Chambolle TV denoising.

## Results (means of 4 synthetic seeds)

| Angular coverage | Method | Whole-volume MAE ↓ | Lesion MAE ↓ | Depth/lateral ROI spread | Projection RMSE ↓ |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ±18° | SART | 0.0253 | 0.2566 | 1.356 | 0.119 |
| ±18° | SART + 3D TV | 0.0267 | 0.2707 | 1.340 | 0.211 |
| ±60° | SART | 0.0109 | 0.1459 | 1.198 | 0.112 |
| ±60° | SART + 3D TV | 0.0122 | 0.1689 | 1.209 | 0.200 |

In this setting a wider acquisition angle reduces reconstruction and lesion errors. The chosen 3D TV proximal step worsens lesion intensity error and projection fit, and must not be described as superior. The ROI depth-to-lateral spread metric uses a **ground-truth-informed local region**, not blinded object detection. Different angular spans with the same 17 views also have different sampling density, so angle coverage is not the only changed property. No statistical significance is asserted with four seeds.

## Limitations / next step
A realistic tomosynthesis simulation needs 3D cone-beam rays from a moving focal spot, a finite 2D detector, calibration of voxel/detector spacing and source-to-detector distance, an adjoint-consistent projector and reconstruction algorithm, and independent validation. The current V7 parallel-ray model excludes these important effects. No clinical images and no ultrasound measurements were used. Projection noise is a simplified Gaussian model rather than photon counting.
